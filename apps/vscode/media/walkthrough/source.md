# Open the source to the side

Click the **Open Source to the Side** button in the editor title of a board, or run
[Open Source to the Side](command:eventStorming.openSource), to open the `.storm` text beside the
canvas. In the text editor, **Open Board to the Side** does the reverse.

Both show the same document: type a line such as `event Order Shipped [900, 300]` and the sticky
appears on the canvas; move it on the canvas and its coordinates change in the text. Undo works on
the document, from either side.
